How to Place Online Fixed Deposits via HLF Digital

Placing Fixed Deposits Online* Securely
Select Your Fixed Deposit Account
Choose an existing fixed deposit account that you have to place the deposit.
Select An Account To Transfer Funds From
Choose your savings account with Hong Leong Finance that you wish to transfer funds from.
Input Your Principal Amount
Enter the amount you wish to deposit.
Set the Tenure
Decide the length of time for your deposit.
Secure With Your PIN
Enter your PIN to secure the transaction.
Transaction Confirmed
Your fixed deposits are now placed securely.

* Fixed deposits placement is available between 9am and 9pm from Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays.
